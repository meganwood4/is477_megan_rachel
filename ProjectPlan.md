# Overview
Our overall goal for this project is to explore how urban planning is associated with crime. We plan to analyze the city of Chicago as our primary research location, utilizing its diverse neighborhoods and various open data resources as a foundation which can later be generalized for other urban cities. We aim to understand how features of urban infrastructure, such as access to public transportation and street lighting, are related to different types of crime. By studying these patterns, we hope to uncover data-driven insights that encourage safer, more equitable city design.
